---
title: Overview Rules
scorers:
  - "project-overview: Checks that project overviews are present, concise, and properly shaped."
  - "app-overview: Checks that app overviews are present, concise, and properly shaped."
  - "package-overview: Checks that package overviews are present, concise, and properly shaped."
---

- Must follow the paragraph count declared by the template for the current note type.
- Must describe the documented surface at a bird's-eye level.
- Must explain the major capabilities of the documented surface and the operational story they create together.
- Must start with a verb.
- May include a short bullet list when a paragraph introduces list-like items such as capability groups or operator surfaces.
- Keep optional opening-block bullets concise and informational, not promotional.
- Important keywords in the opening descriptive block must be in bold.
- Prefer precise product language over promotional or sales-heavy wording.
- Do not describe the documentation structure, folder taxonomy, or navigation inside the opening descriptive block.
- Do not add filler statements about clarity, organization, or where execution starts.
- Do not list module names without explaining what they do together.
