# Nexus Launch Social Plan

## Objective
Offer Nexus for free to early users in exchange for practical product feedback, while supporting the launch with a refreshed landing page and one reusable 6-second video asset.

## Core offer
- Offer: free Nexus access for feedback.
- Ask: users try Nexus on a real workflow and share what confused them, what saved time, and what would make them keep using it.
- Audience: developers, founders, AI-tool power users, productivity-focused builders, and early-stage teams.
- Primary call to action: comment or DM for free access.
- Feedback channel: short form, DM thread, or scheduled 15-minute call.

## Timeline

### Day 1 — Landing page brief
- Define the new landing page promise, audience, CTA, and proof points.
- Draft hero copy focused on Nexus as a custom AI terminal/TUI workflow.
- Identify landing sections: hero, short demo, key workflows, free-feedback offer, FAQ, CTA.
- Output: landing page copy brief and asset checklist.

### Day 2 — Landing page build
- Build the first version of the new landing page.
- Add the free-feedback offer as a clear CTA.
- Reserve a hero/demo slot for the 6-second video.
- Output: reviewable landing page draft.

### Day 3 — Six-second video concept
- Write a simple 6-second storyboard that works on landing and social feeds.
- Suggested structure:
  1. 0–1s: problem hook, e.g. “AI coding workflows are scattered.”
  2. 1–4s: Nexus TUI in action.
  3. 4–6s: CTA, e.g. “Try Nexus free. Give feedback.”
- Output: script, shot list, and capture checklist.

### Day 4 — Video production
- Record, edit, caption, and export the 6-second video.
- Export formats:
  - 16:9 for landing page.
  - 1:1 for X/LinkedIn.
  - 9:16 for shorts/reels/stories.
- Output: final reusable video assets.

### Day 5 — Landing page finalization
- Place the final video on the landing page.
- Tighten copy, CTA, and feedback instructions.
- Verify mobile layout, page speed, and CTA flow.
- Output: publish-ready landing page.

### Day 6 — Social launch prep
- Prepare the first 14 daily posts.
- Create a simple response workflow for comments and DMs.
- Prepare a feedback intake template.
- Output: scheduled social queue and reply snippets.

### Day 7 — Launch
- Publish the new landing page.
- Post the launch announcement with the 6-second video.
- Start daily free-access-for-feedback posts.
- Output: first public launch push.

### Days 8–21 — Daily social posting
- Publish one post per day offering Nexus for free in exchange for feedback.
- Rotate angles: founder build log, developer pain, terminal workflow, AI agents, productivity, early-user invitation, demo clip.
- Reply to every relevant comment and DM within 24 hours.
- Capture objections and questions for landing page and product improvements.
- Output: daily posts, active conversations, feedback notes.

### Day 22 — Review and iterate
- Review social metrics, landing conversion, DMs, and feedback quality.
- Update the landing page based on repeated questions and objections.
- Decide whether to continue the free-feedback offer for another 14 days.
- Output: iteration plan and next content batch.

## Success metrics
- Landing page published by Day 7.
- 6-second video exported in 16:9, 1:1, and 9:16 by Day 4.

## Operating notes
- Keep every post direct and specific.
- Avoid overpromising; present Nexus as early access for feedback.
- Reuse the 6-second video on the landing page and in social posts.
- Turn repeated questions into landing page FAQ updates.
- Turn repeated objections into product or onboarding improvements.
